## Concentration-response modelling in R

SETAC Australasia 2026, Sunday, Room 303 (3rd floor)

| Time | |
|---|---|
| 09:30 | Arrival. Coffee and tea in Room 301, next door. Bring your laptop and set up at a table. |
| 10:00 | Welcome, and an introduction to concentration-response modelling |
| 10:15 | The software stack, and fitting your first model in `bayesnec` |
| 12:30 | Lunch, served one level below |
| 13:15 | Toxicity estimates, model averaging, and choosing a statistical distribution |
| 14:30 | Break. Coffee, tea and snacks in Room 301. |
| 14:45 | Priors, and a worked case study from start to finish |
| 16:00 | Finish, in time for the conference opening ceremony at 16:30 |

The day is hands-on. Bring a laptop, and complete the software setup beforehand:
<https://open-aims.github.io/cr_modelling_training/vignettes/0Software-setup.html>

No time is set aside on the day to install or repair software. If your
installation is not working you will be able to follow the demonstrations, but
not fit models alongside them. Come at 09:30 if you would like it checked before
we start.

The material is at <https://open-aims.github.io/cr_modelling_training/> and stays
available afterwards.
